### **AI领域内常用词和专有词**

* **Attention Mechanism**
    * **音标**: /əˈten.ʃən ˈmek.ə.nɪ.zəm/
    * **英文解释**: A technique that allows a neural network to focus on specific parts of an input sequence when generating an output.
    * **中文解释**: 注意力机制
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * [cite_start]**例句**: The best performing models also connect the encoder and decoder through an **attention mechanism**[cite: 554].

* **Auto-Regressive**
    * **音标**: /ˌɔː.t̬oʊ.rɪˈɡres.ɪv/
    * **英文解释**: A model where the prediction for a given time step is generated based on the outputs from previous time steps.
    * **中文解释**: 自回归
    * **词性**: adjective
    * [cite_start]**例句**: At each step the model is **auto-regressive**, consuming the previously generated symbols as additional input when generating the next[cite: 591].

* **Decoder**
    * **音标**: /diːˈkoʊ.dɚ/
    * **英文解释**: The part of an encoder-decoder model that generates the output sequence from the encoded representation.
    * **中文解释**: 解码器
    * **词性**: noun
    * [cite_start]**例句**: The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks that include an encoder and a **decoder**[cite: 553].

* **Embedding**
    * **音标**: /ɪmˈbed.ɪŋ/
    * **英文解释**: A learned representation for discrete items like words, mapping them to vectors of continuous numbers.
    * **中文解释**: 嵌入
    * **词性**: noun
    * [cite_start]**例句**: To facilitate these residual connections, all sub-layers in the model, as well as the **embedding** layers, produce outputs of dimension dmodel=512[cite: 620].

* **Encoder**
    * **音标**: /ɪnˈkoʊ.dɚ/
    * **英文解释**: The part of an encoder-decoder model that processes the input sequence and converts it into a continuous representation.
    * **中文解释**: 编码器
    * **词性**: noun
    * [cite_start]**例句**: The best performing models also connect the **encoder** and decoder through an attention mechanism[cite: 554].

* **Feed-Forward Network**
    * **音标**: /ˈfiːdˌfɔːr.wɚd ˈnet.wɝːk/
    * **英文解释**: A type of artificial neural network where connections between units do not form a cycle.
    * **中文解释**: 前馈网络
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * [cite_start]**例句**: The first is a multi-head self-attention mechanism, and the second is a simple, position-wise fully connected **feed-forward network**[cite: 617].

* **Multi-Head Attention**
    * **音标**: /ˈmʌl.ti hed əˈten.ʃən/
    * **英文解释**: An attention mechanism that runs multiple attention functions in parallel, allowing the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces.
    * **中文解释**: 多头注意力
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * [cite_start]**例句**: **Multi-head attention** allows the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces at different positions[cite: 666].

* **Positional Encoding**
    * **音标**: /pəˈzɪʃ.ən.əl ɪnˈkoʊ.dɪŋ/
    * **英文解释**: A technique to inject information about the relative or absolute position of tokens in a sequence.
    * **中文解释**: 位置编码
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * [cite_start]**例句**: The **positional encodings** have the same dimension dmodel as the embeddings, so that the two can be summed[cite: 702].

* **Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)**
    * **音标**: /rɪˈkɝː.ənt ˈnʊr.əl ˈnet.wɝːk/
    * **英文解释**: A class of artificial neural networks where connections between nodes form a directed graph along a temporal sequence, allowing it to exhibit temporal dynamic behavior.
    * **中文解释**: 循环神经网络
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * [cite_start]**例句**: **Recurrent neural networks**, long short-term memory and gated recurrent neural networks in particular, have been firmly established as state of the art approaches in sequence modeling...[cite: 566].

* **Self-Attention**
    * **音标**: /self əˈten.ʃən/
    * **英文解释**: An attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation of the sequence.
    * **中文解释**: 自注意力
    * **词性**: noun
    * [cite_start]**例句**: **Self-attention**, sometimes called intra-attention is an attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation of the sequence[cite: 582].

* **Sequence-to-Sequence (seq2seq)**
    * **音标**: /ˈsiː.kwəns.təˈsiː.kwəns/
    * **英文解释**: A class of models that convert sequences from one domain to sequences in another domain, such as in machine translation.
    * **中文解释**: 序列到序列
    * **词性**: adjective
    * [cite_start]**例句**: In contrast to RNN **sequence-to-sequence** models, the Transformer outperforms the BerkeleyParser...[cite: 809].

* **Transduction**
    * **音标**: /trænsˈdʌk.ʃən/
    * **英文解释**: The process of converting an input sequence into an output sequence, as seen in tasks like machine translation.
    * **中文解释**: 转换，转导
    * **词性**: noun
    * [cite_start]**例句**: The dominant sequence **transduction** models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks...[cite: 553].
