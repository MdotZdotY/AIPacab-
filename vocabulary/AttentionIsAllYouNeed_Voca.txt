好的，这是根据《Attention Is All You Need》这篇论文为您整理的、包含四个类别的综合词汇清单。


### **GRE高频词**

* **Dominant**
    * **英文释义**: Most important, powerful, or influential.
    * **中文释义**: 占主导地位的
    * **词性**: adjective
    * **音标**: /ˈdɑː.mə.nənt/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The **dominant** sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks that include an encoder and a decoder.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 主流的序列转换模型是基于复杂的循环或卷积神经网络，其中包括一个编码器和一个解码器。

* **Fundamental**
    * **英文释义**: Forming a necessary base or core; of central importance.
    * **中文释义**: 基础的，根本的
    * **词性**: adjective
    * **音标**: /ˌfʌn.dəˈmen.t̬əl/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The **fundamental** constraint of sequential computation, however, remains.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 然而，顺序计算的根本性制约依然存在。

* **Generalize**
    * **英文释义**: To make a general or broad statement by inferring from specific cases; to apply a model successfully to new data.
    * **中文释义**: 泛化，归纳
    * **词性**: verb
    * **音标**: /ˈdʒen.ə.rəl.aɪz/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: We show that the Transformer **generalizes** well to other tasks by applying it successfully to English constituency parsing both with large and limited training data.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 我们通过将Transformer成功应用于大型和有限训练数据的英语成分句法分析，表明它能很好地泛化到其他任务。

* **Propose**
    * **英文释义**: To put forward an idea or plan for consideration or discussion by others.
    * **中文释义**: 提议，提出
    * **词性**: verb
    * **音标**: /prəˈpoʊz/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: We **propose** a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 我们提出了一种新的、简单的网络架构——Transformer，它完全基于注意力机制，彻底摒弃了循环和卷积。

* **Superior**
    * **英文释义**: Higher in rank, status, or quality.
    * **中文释义**: 更好的，优越的
    * **词性**: adjective
    * **音标**: /səˈpɪr.i.ɚ/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: Experiments on two machine translation tasks show these models to be **superior** in quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 在两项机器翻译任务上的实验表明，这些模型在质量上更为优越，同时更易于并行化，训练所需时间也显著减少。

### **AI领域专有词**

* **Attention Mechanism**
    * **英文释义**: A technique that allows a neural network to focus on specific parts of an input sequence when generating an output.
    * **中文释义**: 注意力机制
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * **音标**: /əˈten.ʃən ˈmek.ə.nɪ.zəm/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The best performing models also connect the encoder and decoder through an **attention mechanism**.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 表现最好的模型还通过一个注意力机制来连接编码器和解码器。

* **Auto-Regressive**
    * **英文释义**: A model where the prediction for a given time step is generated based on the outputs from previous time steps.
    * **中文释义**: 自回归
    * **词性**: adjective
    * **音标**: /ˌɔː.t̬oʊ.rɪˈɡres.ɪv/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: At each step the model is **auto-regressive**, consuming the previously generated symbols as additional input when generating the next.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 在每一步中，模型都是自回归的，它会将先前生成的符号作为额外输入来生成下一个符号。

* **Decoder**
    * **英文释义**: The part of an encoder-decoder model that generates the output sequence from the encoded representation.
    * **中文释义**: 解码器
    * **词性**: noun
    * **音标**: /diːˈkoʊ.dɚ/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks that include an encoder and a **decoder**.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 主流的序列转换模型是基于复杂的循环或卷积神经网络，其中包括一个编码器和一个解码器。

* **Embedding**
    * **英文释义**: A learned representation for discrete items like words, mapping them to vectors of continuous numbers.
    * **中文释义**: 嵌入
    * **词性**: noun
    * **音标**: /ɪmˈbed.ɪŋ/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: To facilitate these residual connections, all sub-layers in the model, as well as the **embedding** layers, produce outputs of dimension dmodel=512.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 为了方便这些残差连接，模型中所有的子层以及嵌入层都产生维度为dmodel=512的输出。

* **Encoder**
    * **英文释义**: The part of an encoder-decoder model that processes the input sequence and converts it into a continuous representation.
    * **中文释义**: 编码器
    * **词性**: noun
    * **音标**: /ɪnˈkoʊ.dɚ/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The best performing models also connect the **encoder** and decoder through an attention mechanism.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 表现最好的模型还通过一个注意力机制来连接编码器和解码器。

* **Feed-Forward Network**
    * **英文释义**: A type of artificial neural network where connections between units do not form a cycle.
    * **中文释义**: 前馈网络
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * **音标**: /ˈfiːdˌfɔːr.wɚd ˈnet.wɝːk/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The first is a multi-head self-attention mechanism, and the second is a simple, position-wise fully connected **feed-forward network**.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 第一个是多头自注意力机制，第二个是简单的、按位置全连接的前馈网络。

* **Multi-Head Attention**
    * **英文释义**: An attention mechanism that runs multiple attention functions in parallel, allowing the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces.
    * **中文释义**: 多头注意力
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * **音标**: /ˈmʌl.ti hed əˈten.ʃən/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: **Multi-head attention** allows the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces at different positions.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 多头注意力使得模型能够在不同位置上共同关注来自不同表示子空间的信息。

* **Positional Encoding**
    * **英文释义**: A technique to inject information about the relative or absolute position of tokens in a sequence.
    * **中文释义**: 位置编码
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * **音标**: /pəˈzɪʃ.ən.əl ɪnˈkoʊ.dɪŋ/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The **positional encodings** have the same dimension dmodel as the embeddings, so that the two can be summed.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 位置编码与嵌入具有相同的维度dmodel，因此两者可以相加。

* **Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)**
    * **英文释义**: A class of artificial neural networks where connections between nodes form a directed graph along a temporal sequence, allowing it to exhibit temporal dynamic behavior.
    * **中文释义**: 循环神经网络
    * **词性**: noun phrase
    * **音标**: /rɪˈkɝː.ənt ˈnʊr.əl ˈnet.wɝːk/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: **Recurrent neural networks**, long short-term memory and gated recurrent neural networks in particular, have been firmly established as state of the art approaches in sequence modeling....
    * **例句中文翻译**: 循环神经网络，特别是长短期记忆网络和门控循环神经网络，已被公认为是在序列建模上的最佳方法....

* **Self-Attention**
    * **英文释义**: An attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation of the sequence.
    * **中文释义**: 自注意力
    * **词性**: noun
    * **音标**: /self əˈten.ʃən/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: **Self-attention**, sometimes called intra-attention is an attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation of the sequence.
    * **例句中文翻译**: 自注意力，有时也称为内部注意力，是一种关联单个序列不同位置以计算该序列表示的注意力机制。

* **Sequence-to-Sequence (seq2seq)**
    * **英文释义**: A class of models that convert sequences from one domain to sequences in another domain, such as in machine translation.
    * **中文释义**: 序列到序列
    * **词性**: adjective
    * **音标**: /ˈsiː.kwəns.təˈsiː.kwəns/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: In contrast to RNN **sequence-to-sequence** models, the Transformer outperforms the BerkeleyParser....
    * **例句中文翻译**: 与循环神经网络的序列到序列模型相比，Transformer的表现优于伯克利解析器....

* **Transduction**
    * **英文释义**: The process of converting an input sequence into an output sequence, as seen in tasks like machine translation.
    * **中文释义**: 转换，转导
    * **词性**: noun
    * **音标**: /trænsˈdʌk.ʃən/
    * **在论文中的例句 (英文)**: The dominant sequence **transduction** models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks....
    * **例句中文翻译**: 主流的序列转换模型是基于复杂的循环或卷积神经网络....
